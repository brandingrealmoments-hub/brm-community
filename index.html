BRMMONEY Community
BRM credits are issued only after Square confirms a successful $5.00 USD payment. Each confirmed $5.00 package adds 50 BRM at a redemption value of $0.10 per BRM. Credits are for eligible marketplace products only; transfers and cash-out are not offered, subject to applicable law. Marketplace listings are sourced from your approved Square catalog. Community activity figures shown above and in the chart are live rolling 7-day totals from Supabase; a zero means no matching activity was recorded for that period. BRM credits are not publicly traded cryptocurrency or an investment.

Program rules and reward settings are still being finalized and are not a final legal policy. Credit expiration is currently disabled. Refund and chargeback events are sent for manual review; automatic reversals are disabled.
